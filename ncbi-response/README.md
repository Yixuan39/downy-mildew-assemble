# NCBI contamination review

Evidence and reply for the SC1982 foreign-contamination flags in submission SUB16446400. The final assembly removes two flagged bacterial contigs and retains six internal fungal-affinity spans after review.

- `ncbi_reply.Rmd` and `ncbi_reply.pdf`: response letter.
- `scripts/`: read mapping, BLASTP and CIGAR-support commands.
- `data/`: flagged spans and supporting evidence tables.
- `figures/`: coverage figure.
